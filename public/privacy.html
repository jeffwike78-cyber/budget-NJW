Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Budget App is a private, personal budgeting tool used by our household. It is not offered to the public and does not sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone. This page explains what data the app touches and why.

What data the app uses

  • Bank & card data (via Plaid): With your permission, the app connects to your financial institutions through Plaid to import account balances and transactions so they can be categorized into budget envelopes.
  • Email (via Google / Gmail): If you connect a Gmail account, the app uses read-only access to search for purchase receipts that match a transaction, so it can fill in what a charge was for. It reads only what is needed to find and summarize a matching receipt. It never sends, deletes, or modifies email.
  • Budget data you enter: Envelopes, categories, notes, and manual entries you create in the app.

How data is stored

Data is stored in a private database (Supabase) accessible only to the signed-in members of this household. Access tokens for connected services are stored securely on the server and are never exposed to the browser.

How Google user data is used

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used solely to locate and summarize receipts matching your own transactions. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with third parties, and it is not used to train generalized AI models.

Revoking access

You can disconnect a bank or Gmail account at any time from the Accounts page in the app. You can also revoke the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
